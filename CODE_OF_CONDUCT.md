# Code of conduct

We want Linvesther to be a place where people can contribute without fear of
harassment or contempt.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, give specific and kind
feedback, and accept it the same way. Criticize code and ideas, never people.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks,
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of
discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that go
against this code, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious
violations. To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on this
repository or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
